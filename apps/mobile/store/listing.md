# Cyrelle store listing notes

## Summary

Cyrelle is a global community for women. Basic is free. Premium is an optional subscription. Business profiles are free. Business Blue Tick is a separate optional subscription. The app contains advertisements for both Basic and Premium.

## Sign-in

Email or Google. The app does not ask for a phone number to create an account, sign in, or reset a password.

## Age

There is no universal 18+ gate. If a country configuration sets a minimum age, that country is the only place the rule applies.

## Subscriptions

- Premium, monthly and yearly. India reference price: ₹499 per month or ₹5,000 per year. Other countries use configured regional prices.
- Business Blue Tick, monthly and yearly, billed separately.
- Premium does not remove ads and does not include Business Blue Tick.
- Account deletion is available in profile settings. The API removes personal content and verification documents. Payment and appointment records may be retained where the law requires it.

## Permissions

- Camera: posts, stories, reels, live, and verification selfies.
- Microphone: reels, live, and optional emergency voice messages.
- Photos: only when the member picks media to post.
- Location: only when the member shares a place or an emergency location.
- Notifications: messages, appointments, safety, and account alerts.

## Emergency

The Safety Hub can record an SOS and email emergency contacts the member added. It shows the official number for the member’s country. It does not silently place calls or send SMS. Shake SOS and Auto SOS are off until enabled. Auto SOS cannot detect every emergency.

## Payments

Card numbers are handled by the configured payment provider. Cyrelle does not store raw card data. Appointment payments include a 15% platform commission on eligible completed bookings.

## Moderation

Members can report, block, mute, and hide content. Admins review reports. Advertising is labeled Sponsored, Advertisement, or Promoted.

## Privacy

Identity documents are private. Private messages are not sold or used as ad inventory.
